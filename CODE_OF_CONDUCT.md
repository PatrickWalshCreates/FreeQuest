# Code of conduct

FreeQuest is made by people who like making games, for people who want to make games. Everyone is
welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone asks a "basic" question at some point.
- Disagree about ideas, never about people. Assume good intent, and explain your reasoning.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive, on code, issues and games alike.
- Respect people's time and boundaries: no spam, no repeated pinging, no unwanted contact.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery aimed at people, or threats.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainers privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to reach them). Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may ban people from the
project's spaces for behaviour that breaks this code. This applies in the repository's issues,
pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.
